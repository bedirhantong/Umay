Privacy policy

Umay (com.appvalence.umay) · Last updated: 10 October 2026 · Türkçe is the original text.

In short

  • Umay asks for no account. It collects no name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • Camera frames are processed on the device only, at the moment they are seen. They are not recorded, stored or sent.
  • Umay shows no ads and does no usage analytics. Google ML Kit, used to read signs, sends its own performance metrics to Google (see Third parties).
  • Maps, settings and logs stay on your device. Data leaves it only when you choose to publish a map or share a file yourself.
  • Spoken questions are transcribed by your phone's on-device speech recognizer; audio is not sent over the internet.
  • For institutions there is a separate build that never connects to the internet (see below).

Who we are

Umay is an open-source project by Bedirhan Tong (AppValence). The source code is public on GitHub, so everything below can be checked against the code. See the support page to get in touch.

Spoken questions

On Android 12 and later, “Ask something” uses only your phone's on-device speech recognizer. If the phone has none, or no Turkish language pack, the feature says so and does not work; it never falls back to recognition over the internet. On Android 13 and later a missing Turkish pack is downloaded by the recognition service's own app; the download contains none of your speech. Android 11 and earlier have no on-device recognizer interface, so the request carries Android's “offline recognition only” flag; honouring it is up to the recognition service on the phone. The transcript is matched only against the building map on the device and then discarded. Umay stores neither the audio nor the text.

Spoken guidance uses only a Turkish voice installed on the phone that needs no internet. If the phone's speech engine falls back to a voice that reads over the internet, Umay says so and asks for the offline voice to be installed.

Data stored on your device

  • Building maps: maps you surveyed or imported (place names, floor information, Bluetooth signal strengths recorded at named places and along the walked path, and a wall map if it was surveyed with the camera).
  • Unfinished survey: a draft kept on the device while a map is being surveyed, so the walk is not lost if the app is closed. It is deleted when the survey is cleared and is not backed up.
  • Settings: speech rate, step length and similar preferences.
  • Field log (off by default): if you turn it on in the Files screen, indoor position, steps, signal strength, air pressure and the instructions given are written to a file during guidance. It contains no images or audio. The file does not leave your device until you share it.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, the stack trace is written to a file on the device. It is never sent automatically; you can share it if you wish.

Maps and settings may be included in your Google account backup if Android's own backup is turned on. Field logs and crash reports are excluded from backup. This is an Android feature; no data goes to a Umay server.

You can delete all of this from the Files screen, or by uninstalling the app.

Shared map pool

This feature is optional. Browsing and downloading buildings in the pool requires no sign-in. When you choose to make a map public, the following is uploaded:

  • the building name, floor count, point count and file size,
  • the map itself: the place names you gave, indoor coordinates and Bluetooth signal information,
  • the update time and a randomly generated anonymous identifier.

An uploaded map can be read and downloaded by anyone. Do not put personal information (your name, home address) in building or place names. The app asks for confirmation before publishing.

Publishing uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication; no e-mail, name or phone number is requested. The anonymous identifier is not tied to anything that identifies you and exists only so that you can remove the map later. You can unpublish from Files > Shared map pool; only the device that uploaded the map can do this. See the data deletion page (in Turkish) for what to do if that is not possible.

Third parties

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Anonymous Authentication): only when you use the shared map pool. Google may process technical data such as the IP address under its own policy.
  • Google ML Kit text recognition: reads sign text on the device; images and the text read do not leave the device. According to Google, ML Kit sends metrics about the performance and use of its APIs to Google and occasionally contacts Google's servers for model updates.
  • ARCore (only when surveying with the camera): Camera mapping runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. It is not used on the phone of the person being guided.
  • Your phone's speech recognition and text-to-speech services: used in their on-device mode, as described above.

Umay uses no ad network, no usage analytics and no crash-reporting service.

Institution build

Institutions can use “Umay Kurum” for their own buildings (com.appvalence.umay.kurum). It has no internet permission; it contains no Firebase and no shared map pool; Android backup and device-to-device transfer are off. Maps move between phones as files. These guarantees are checked automatically at build time. Details are in GIZLILIK.md.

Your rights

You can ask whether data about you is processed, request information, and request correction or deletion. You can remove a map you published from within the app. If that is not possible, follow the steps on the data deletion page. If you are in Turkey, Article 11 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK) applies.

Children

Umay is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
