## App name

Umay: Indoor Guidance Aid

## Short description

Audio and vibration guidance indoors for blind people. Open source.

## Full description

Umay is an open-source indoor guidance aid for blind and low-vision people. Where GPS does not work, it guides you to your destination inside a building with spoken directions and vibration patterns.

The app's interface and spoken guidance are in Turkish. Other languages are not available yet.

IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTE
Umay is an aid, not a mobility device. It does not replace a white cane or a guide dog. The camera only detects people in front of you, and it can miss one. Stairs, doors and ramps are known only if the person who mapped the building marked them. The position estimate can drift by a few metres at times. Always rely on your own attention and your cane.

THE BUILDING NEEDS BLUETOOTH BEACONS
Umay works in buildings that have been mapped. For a reliable position the building must have Bluetooth beacons. Without beacons the app can only count steps, and the position drifts over time. A building is mapped once, by walking through it with the Beacon Scanner screen.

HOW IT WORKS
GPS does not work indoors, so Umay combines two sources:
- Step counting: steps are computed from the phone's accelerometer and gyroscope. The compass is not used, because steel and cables in buildings make it unreliable.
- Bluetooth fingerprints: beacon signals at the mapped points correct the drift that step counting builds up.

FEATURES
- Spoken directions: step-by-step guidance with a Turkish speech voice. Speech rate is adjustable. Repeat the last instruction with one tap.
- Vibration patterns: every kind of instruction has its own pattern. Try them all in advance from the guide in Settings.
- Camera warning for people: the phone camera detects people directly in front of you and warns you. Images are processed on the device and never recorded.
- Guidance across floors: finds routes across floors, including stairs and elevators. If the phone has a barometer, floor changes are detected automatically.
- Read the text around you: reads sign text on the device and speaks it.
- Ask something: answers questions you ask by voice using the building's own map.
- Works with the screen off: guidance continues while the phone is in your pocket. The camera cannot run with the screen off; position and directions continue.
- Large, fixed-position touch targets, designed for use without looking at the screen.

SHARING MAPS
You can move a map you made to other devices as a file. You can also add it to the shared map pool so that others going to the same building do not have to map it again. A map in the pool can be seen by anyone, and you can remove it at any time.

PRIVACY
- No account is needed.
- Camera images are not recorded, stored or sent.
- Your GPS location is not collected.
- There are no ads and no usage analytics.
- Maps, settings and logs stay on your device. Data leaves it only when you share a map or send a file yourself.

PERMISSIONS
- Camera: to detect people in front of you and read signs.
- Location and Bluetooth: Android requires the location permission to scan for Bluetooth beacons. Umay does not use your GPS location.
- Microphone: only to listen to your question when you tap "Ask something". Audio is not recorded.
- Notifications: so guidance can keep running with the screen off.
